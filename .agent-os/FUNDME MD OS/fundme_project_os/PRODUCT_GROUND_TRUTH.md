## Big idea

FundMe is a conversational funding-readiness and founder-optimization platform.

A founder provides minimal information, uploads or pastes supporting material, and explains the startup naturally through voice or text. FundMe builds a structured evidence dossier, asks only the questions that materially affect the assessment, and produces a sharp, credible report of how the founder and startup currently read to funding decision-makers.

FundMe then helps fix the weaknesses it identified.

## Long-term product sequence

```text
diagnose → improve → match → apply → track → automate
```

## Current wedge

```text
diagnose → download/share → referral early access → improve
```

## Primary user

An early-stage founder preparing to approach:

- accelerators
- incubators
- fellowships
- grants
- competitions
- angel investors
- seed investors

Typical state:

- scattered founder and startup information
- incomplete or inconsistent story
- unclear founder-market fit
- repeated application work
- uncertainty about what reviewers actually notice
- a website, deck, resume, profile or notes, but no structured dossier
- distrust of generic AI advice

## Primary pains

1. Application forms feel like exams.
2. Founders repeatedly rewrite the same story.
3. The website, deck and founder profile often conflict.
4. Founders cannot objectively identify credibility gaps.
5. Traction claims often lack timeframe, source or context.
6. Founders do not know what to fix first.
7. Opportunity discovery is noisy and brand-led rather than fit-led.
8. Generic AI advice is confident but not evidence-grounded.

## Core promise

> See how your startup and founder profile read before you apply—and understand exactly what to fix next.

## Product experience

The product must feel like a skilled founder mentor actively understanding the startup.

It must not feel like:

- a long form
- a school test
- a blank generic chat
- a dashboard with no earned value
- a gimmicky insult generator
- a funding-probability calculator

## Canonical V1 flow

```text
Landing
→ Start my funding assessment
→ Minimal founder/startup details
→ Add website, deck, profile, resume or notes
→ Speak or type to the FundMe mentor
→ Confirm extracted facts
→ Answer only high-impact missing questions
→ Analysis
→ Funding Readiness report
→ Download/share
→ Join optimization early access
→ Referral progression
```

## Assessment output

- Overall Funding Readiness Score
- Evidence coverage
- Confidence
- Founder credibility
- Founder-market fit
- Problem clarity
- Solution clarity
- Market quality
- Differentiation
- Product maturity
- Traction and proof
- Funding narrative
- Pitch-deck readiness
- Founder/LinkedIn positioning
- Strengths
- Red flags
- Contradictions
- Unsupported claims
- Missing evidence
- Three biggest reasons the startup may fail
- Three highest-leverage actions

The score is not a probability of receiving funding.

## Shock value

Shock comes from accurate recognition and precise evidence gaps.

Good:

> You cite ₹8 lakh in revenue, but the deck does not state whether it is monthly, annual or cumulative. A reviewer cannot judge traction quality.

Bad:

> Investors will never fund you.

## Competitor pattern policy

Learn from competitors without copying protected expression.

Useful patterns:

- fast free diagnostic
- visible sample output
- specific category scores
- time-to-value
- outcome-first positioning
- clear progression from diagnosis to execution
- privacy reassurance
- concrete next action

Do not copy branding, proprietary wording, scoring logic or unsupported claims.
