## Mission

Make startup applications as intelligent, reusable, and operational as a modern sales pipeline - without turning founders into spam applicants.

## Vision

A founder should be able to give Fundme their existing startup context once, understand what is weak, see where they genuinely fit, create stronger applications, and track every opportunity from one trusted workspace.

## Product definition

**Fundme is the application intelligence and execution layer for founders.**

It turns fragmented founder and startup material into a reusable dossier, diagnoses readiness, matches the startup to relevant opportunities, drafts application-specific answers, and tracks outcomes.

## Primary user

Early-stage founders who:

- are pre-seed or seed stage;
- are applying to 5-30 programs over the next 30-90 days;
- already have some startup material but do not trust its quality;
- are targeting accelerators, incubators, fellowships, competitions, structured grants, and startup perks;
- do not have a chief of staff or application operations team;
- want leverage without surrendering control.

## Jobs to be done

1. Tell me how my startup and founder story reads to a decision-maker.
2. Show me what is missing or weak before I apply.
3. Tell me which opportunities are actually worth my time.
4. Reuse my verified context without rewriting from zero.
5. Help me create a tailored, credible application.
6. Keep my applications, deadlines, responses, and next actions organized.

## Product wedge

The front-door wedge is diagnosis, not automation.

`diagnose -> improve -> match -> draft -> track`

Automation and network effects are earned later after trust, structured data, and repeat usage exist.

## What Fundme is not

- Not merely a startup-program directory.
- Not a generic AI writer.
- Not a black-box startup score.
- Not a mass investor-outreach tool.
- Not an application spam bot.
- Not a broad grants bureaucracy engine in the MVP.
- Not founder entertainment.

## Positioning

**Primary positioning:** One startup dossier. Better applications everywhere.

**Expanded positioning:** Fundme helps founders stress-test, improve, and deploy their startup story across the right opportunities.

## Competitive boundary

Fundraisly focuses on investor discovery, warm-path mapping, outreach, and booked investor meetings. VC Boom focuses on deck scoring, investor matching, and personalized investor outreach. Fundme should avoid becoming a smaller version of either. Its differentiated territory is accelerator, incubator, fellowship, grant, perk, and program application intelligence - with a reusable evidence-backed dossier and program-specific execution workspace.

## Defensibility

1. **Opportunity graph:** structured requirements, eligibility, questions, deadlines, benefits, selection emphasis, sources, and freshness.
2. **Founder/startup evidence graph:** verified facts, source fragments, confidence, missing evidence, and history.
3. **Application intelligence:** mappings between repeated question types, verified facts, program emphasis, and outcomes.
4. **Outcome learning:** submitted, shortlisted, interviewed, rejected, accepted, and funded outcomes improve the system.
5. **Workflow lock-in:** dossier, reports, drafts, versions, deadlines, and application history become expensive to recreate elsewhere.
6. **India plus global depth:** serious coverage of Indian ecosystem programs alongside global accelerators and startup perks.

## Product principles

- Evidence before confidence.
- Rules before model opinion.
- Quality before application volume.
- User control before automation.
- Curated and fresh before massive and stale.
- Functional core before cosmetic expansion.
- One source of truth before agent autonomy.

## North-star metric

**Weekly founders completing an actionable funding workflow:** a completed diagnosis plus at least one meaningful improvement, qualified match, or application draft.
